[Open Pages CMS](https://app.pagescms.org/Nightingalelyy/nightingalelyy.github.io/main)

Sign in with your GitHub account to edit articles, update pages, and upload images.

Choose **Articles** to update an existing article or create a new one. Choose
**Homepage** to edit the landing page, or **Site settings** to change the site title
and description.

Saving in the editor publishes your changes after the website finishes rebuilding.
